Adjustable solar post mount

20 W panel on a 100 × 100 hardwood post · 4 seasonal tilt positions · all dimensions in mm

Panel: Powertech ZM9094, 639 × 294 × 23 mm, mounted portrait (long side running down the slope). Its Z-brackets are 100 mm long, fixed by one screw each at 160 mm from the top and bottom edges (319 between centres), and measure 357 across the outside edges. Each pair of bracket feet screws down onto a 140 × 19 cross rail, wide enough to take both holes in each foot.

1. Seasonal positions

Geometry: the prop pivots 300 mm along the arm and is 300 mm between hole centres, so each peg hole is 600 × sin(tilt) below the hinge. Tilts are set for the sun's average height over each season: latitude − 15° (summer), − 7° (spring), + 7° (autumn), + 15° (winter).

10. Drilling tips

  1. Drill the hinge hole in the post first, then the arms, spacers and props.
  2. Assemble with the hinge and pivots only. Swing the panel to 30° using a phone inclinometer on the arm, then drill the spring hole through the props into the post. Repeat for the other three angles. This absorbs any small drilling errors.
  3. Through 100 mm of hardwood, drill halfway from each side so the holes meet in the middle and stay square.
  4. Drill the panel bolt holes in the rails with the Z-brackets clamped in place as a template, after the rails are screwed to the arms.
  5. Leave the hinge nuts just snug enough that the arms swing with a firm push. Only the peg's wing nut needs undoing to change season.
